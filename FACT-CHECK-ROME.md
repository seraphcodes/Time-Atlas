# TimeAtlas: Rome, AD 100 — fact-check

Session 1, 25 November 2026. Every claim shown on screen, what supports it and how sure we are. Same method as `FACT-CHECK.md` for Giza: at least two independent sources per figure; books cited on screen were reached through their online editions (Platner & Ashby on LacusCurtius/Heidelberg) or through sources quoting them. **Claridge and Coarelli were not read directly in this session**; session 4 should spot-check the marker texts against them.

## Session 4: pages, credits and text pass (25 November 2026)

**Correction:** the Basilica Aemilia card said "Rebuilt AD 14–22". The building burned in 14 BC, was rebuilt by Augustus in the name of the Aemilius family, and was repaired again in AD 22 (Platner & Ashby; Stanford Forma Urbis project). Card and ledger now say so. Pliny's "three most beautiful buildings" is "in the world" (Platner & Ashby), not "in Rome".
