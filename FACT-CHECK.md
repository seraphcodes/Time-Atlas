# TimeAtlas: Giza — fact-check

Checked on 11 November 2026. Every number and claim shown on screen, with what supports it, what I changed, and what is still uncertain.

**How this was checked.** Each claim was compared against at least two independent sources found on the web: the PNAS and Physical Review Letters papers (abstracts and full text where reachable), Britannica, the Egyptian Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities, Wikipedia (as a pointer to the primary literature), and news coverage quoting the researchers. **The books cited on screen (Lehner, Petrie, Arnold, Tallet) were not read directly.** They are the standard references for these figures, and the secondary sources agree with them, but a human spot-check against the books is still worth doing before calling this "academically reviewed".

## Simplifications that are disclosed on screen

- The capstone is plain limestone because its original look is unknown.
- Khafre's and Menkaure's positions are approximate, and the whole Giza layout is a sketch of the real plateau.
- The Nile valley is drawn narrower than it is, so the river shift is 1.4 km rather than seven or eight.
- Cairo is a block model, not real buildings.
- The lamp in the King's Chamber is an invention.

## Not modelled

The Great Sphinx, the subterranean chamber, the relieving chambers above the King's Chamber, the narrow shafts, the boat pits, the pyramid causeways and temples (except as sketches), and the workers' town and cemeteries.

## Still open (needs a human or a library)

1. Read Lehner's *The Complete Pyramids*, Petrie's survey and Arnold's *Building in Egypt* for the exact passages behind the interior dimensions.
2. Interior sizes in the 3D model were rounded from Petrie; a second source for the King's Chamber's measurements would be good.
3. If the site is ever shown to schools or museums, ask an Egyptologist to review the ramp, harbour and silting statements.
