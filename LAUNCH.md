## Before you post

- Deploy first, then run the address through LinkedIn's Post Inspector so the preview image shows.
- Record a 20 to 30 second screen video on your laptop: scroll from the harbour to the pyramid, drag the Then/Now slider, tap one marker. A video gets far more reach than a link alone.
- Post the link in the first comment if you want the post itself to travel further (LinkedIn tends to favour posts without outbound links).

## Post A: the build story (English)

I wanted to see what the Great Pyramid looked like when it was new.

So I built TimeAtlas: you scroll, and you travel to Giza in 2560 BC. A harbour at the foot of the plateau, a ramp, wet sand to slide the stone, white limestone casing that no longer exists. A Then/Now slider turns the pyramid back into today's ruin.

A few things I cared about:
- Every marker you tap has sources, and says what is documented and what is a reconstruction.
- It runs in the browser, with no install and no tracking.
- It works with a keyboard, with reduced motion, and without WebGL.

Built with React, three.js and GSAP as a Digital Business & Data Science student. Giza is the first stop; I would like to add more places and times.

Try it: <LINK>
